# Product Guidelines - PhoneDetective`n`n## Brand Voice & Tone`n- **Voice:** Friendly, supportive, and transparent.`n- **Tone:** Helpful and reassuring. Technical jargon should be explained clearly to ensure the app feels accessible to everyone, not just power users.`n- **Communication Goal:** Empower users by making complex device data easy to understand and act upon.`n`n## Visual Identity`n- **Design System:** Material Design 3 (Material You).`n- **Aesthetic:** Modern, clean, and adaptive. Utilize dynamic color themes that respond to the user's wallpaper.`n- **Iconography:** Use rounded, approachable icons. Avoid overly sharp or aggressive imagery.`n- **Typography:** Prioritize readability. Use standard system fonts (Roboto/Google Sans) with clear hierarchies.`n`n## Interaction Principles`n- **Clarity over Complexity:** Present critical information first; allow users to drill down for more detail.`n- **Feedback:** Provide immediate and helpful feedback for all user actions (e.g., clear success/error states for diagnostics).`n- **Consistency:** Follow standard Android navigation patterns to minimize the learning curve.
